# Art direction

**Rule.** Every illustration (banner, scene, icon, badge, notification or deal art) follows
`docs/ART-DIRECTION.md`: a canon banner attached to the generation request, its prompt block
pasted in, its checklist passed, and the result shown to the founder before it replaces anything
in the app. A banner's layers are never cut out and reused alone (a sticker, an icon, another
view): only whole standalone pictures are reused.

**Why.** Generated assets drifted again and again (gem facets, gloss, generic icon packs) and were
redone in loops; the founder asked for the style to be written down once, clearly.

**How.** Read `docs/ART-DIRECTION.md` before generating. Propose first (what will be drawn), generate
a sheet, show it, integrate only after a yes. A new recurring object gets its description added to
the doc.
