# Worklog — v2.36.1 (in progress)

## Added
- **Supporter badge (Ko-fi, Buy Me a Coffee, PayPal).** A payment on any of the three tied to an account gives it a golden-heart **Supporter** badge on the sidebar avatar and a thank-you notification — a thank-you, never a feature. Tied by the account's fixed **supporter code** `TS-XXXX-XXXX` the support menu shows (copy chip: "add your code to your payment message"; derived from the uid so it never changes, unlike the friend code — which still works as a fallback), else by the payer's email; anyone else claims it from the menu ("Already gave? Get your badge": payer email or transaction id). A supporter's menu thanks them instead of pitching.
  - Backend (`TigerTag_Firebase_Backend`): `functions/supportWebhooks.js` — `kofiWebhook` (Ko-fi `verification_token` vs secret `KOFI_VERIFICATION_TOKEN`), `bmcWebhook` (HMAC-SHA256 `x-signature-sha256` vs secret `BMC_WEBHOOK_SECRET`; both BMC payload shapes), `paypalIpn` (PayPal IPN, verified by post-back; completed incoming payments only, note in `memo`) — all record `supportDonations/{source}_{transactionId}` idempotently — and `claimSupport` (ID token; matches an unclaimed payment by lower-cased email or transaction id → `granted`/`not_found`/`taken`); `claimSupport { action: "code" }` returns the supporter code and registers it in `supporterCodes/{code}` (server-only); all set the server-only `users/{uid}.supporter = { since, lastAt, sources, monthly, count }` and, the first time, a `supporter_thanks` notification. `firestore.rules`: `supporter` joins `roles` / `tier` as refused from clients; `supportDonations` has no client rule. README documents both.
  - Studio: `state.supporter` (user doc + local-first cache, reset on sign-out), `renderSupporterBadge()`, supporter-code chip (`_supporterCode`, cached per account in `tigertag.supporterCode.<uid>`) + claim form in `_openCoffee` (`_claimSupport` → `claimSupport`), `supporter_thanks` row in the notification center — `renderer/inventory.js`, `renderer/css/10-settings.css`, `docs/firestore-schema.md`
- **Support the project: Buy Me a Coffee, Ko-fi or PayPal.** Every coffee button (sidebar, About, What's New, the requests card's coffee cards) opens a small chooser anchored next to it — Buy Me a Coffee (card, Apple / Google Pay), Ko-fi (`ko-fi.com/tigersystemio`, card or PayPal, one-off or monthly) or PayPal (`paypal.me/tigersystemio`) — for supporters who can't or won't use BMC; closes on pick, outside click or Esc. The repo's GitHub **Sponsor** button lists all three (`.github/FUNDING.yml`) — `renderer/inventory.js` (`_openCoffee`, `PAYPAL_URL`, `KOFI_URL`), `renderer/report/index.js`, `renderer/css/10-settings.css`, `assets/svg/logos/logo_paypal.svg`, `assets/svg/logos/logo_kofi.svg`
  - Reassures before it asks: it says the app is free and open source for good, built after hours by one maker with the community, a footer notes that payment happens on the platform's site (the card never reaches us) and offers a free alternative, a GitHub star. The About and What's New buttons are a neutral cup + "Support" pill instead of the Buy Me a Coffee wordmark (they open the three-platform chooser); support buttons get localised aria-labels; the requests card's coffee line drops the repeated "coffee" — `renderer/inventory.js` (`_openCoffee`, `GITHUB_REPO_URL`), `renderer/inventory.html`, `renderer/css/10-settings.css`, `renderer/css/70-detail-misc.css` (`.support-btn` replaces `.bmc-btn`), `renderer/css/60-modals.css`
  - Popover per the redesign mockup: title with a red heart, a short reassuring intro (free and open source for good), a caret pointing at the button that opened it, smart placement (right of the button when there is room, otherwise above, then below), the opener lit with a yellow ring while open, a ↗ hint on the hovered platform, lift-on-hover, 150 ms open / 120 ms close (off under reduced motion), options left-aligned (a global button rule was centring them); the sidebar cup is solid coffee-yellow in the dark theme (`logo_buy_me_coffee_yellow.svg`); opened from the requests side card it sits to the right of the whole card (the topic list stays visible), or becomes a compact centred modal over a dimmed backdrop when there is no room
