## Fixed
- **A deleted spool no longer stays on screen** (founder: "je n'arrive pas à supprimer"): the delta sync (`_listenSynced`) only listens to documents whose `syncAt` is newer than the session, so the delete of an older one reached no listener and the spool stayed until a full reload. Every Firestore delete made (a ref, or a batch on commit) now drops the document from the delta-synced copy it belongs to at once, without waiting for the server (`_watchSyncedDeletes` → `_syncForget`), and the spool card's Delete closes the card before the batch is confirmed (`_wireToolbox`). Measured on the founder's account with the delete button (Matte Cinnabar twin): card closed and spool gone 0.34 s after the 1.5 s hold, where it used to stay on screen until the server confirmed (54 s behind a queue of writes); server confirmation 3 s, in the background.
- **A spool added from its chip is written whole** (founder's speed test, 2026-11-13): `_writeChipDoc` now writes `productKey` and `protocol` with the chip's doc (`normalizeRow` + `_productKeyHash`), so `syncSpoolMirrors` no longer follows each new spool with up to three update batches, each waiting its turn in Firestore's write queue (a write took 1.7–3 s to be confirmed here). Re-adding the Matte Cinnabar from the TigerPOD: "Added" 0.84 s after opening the view, its card in the inventory at 1.3 s, 2 writes instead of 6. Moving spools between rack slots was measured too and needs nothing: the slots swap on screen in 74–310 ms, the server confirms in about 1 s.
