Filters written right in the search bar, a colour search in the catalogue, and fixes for the arrival sound, the Creality board camera and the detached camera wall.

## Added

- **Filters in the search bar.** The Brand, Material, Aspect, Type, Tag, List, Weight and Rack filters (and Diameter) are now pills written in the search field. Click the field or type a field name to add one, pick its values, and combine as many as you like. The TigerTag, TigerTag+, TigerData and TigerData+ tiles in the header set the Type filter. In the catalogue the filters are Brand, Series, Material, Aspect and Type.
- **Colour search in the catalogue.** A new colour button in the search bar of the Search views finds products by colour: type a HEX code, a RAL Classic code or name (in your own language), or click a point of a photo you drop in. The closest products come first, and each picture shows its colour distance (ΔE). The colour is a pill you can edit or remove.
- **Detached camera wall shows your IP cameras**: the camera chosen in place of a printer's own, and the cameras linked to no printer (RTSP, MJPEG and WebRTC), each window with its own stream.

## Changed

- While a chip flagged to update is rewritten on the TigerPOD, the spool card opens and its update banner fills and reads "Update in progress…".
- The catalogue bundled with the app is up to date (22,500 products), with refreshed brand and material tables.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where opening the catalogue after an update played a burst of "spool added" sounds for spools you already had.
- Fixed an issue where the Creality camera on the printers board did not start when you pressed play, or when it started automatically. Tested on a K2.
- Fixed an issue where the detached camera wall showed a "502 Bad Gateway" card instead of your IP cameras.
- Fixed an issue where a spool whose photo changed in the catalogue kept its old picture. The catalogue is now checked more often, and when you open the spool.
- "Link the proprietary Tags" is now offered only for brands that ship chips of their own (Bambu Lab, Snapmaker, ELEGOO, Anycubic, Creality, QIDI Tech, Prusament).
- Fixed an issue where a chip that could not be rewritten on the TigerPOD overwrote the spool's record with the chip's old values. The record is kept and the chip is rewritten the next time it is presented.
- Fixed an issue where a catalogue update reached only one chip of a twin spool.
- Fixed an issue where a TigerTag that was moved during reading opened the new-chip card.
- Chip backups are now kept for TigerTag+ Certified chips only, and restoring a `.ttag` no longer overwrites a chip already on your account.

## Removed

- The Brand, Material, Aspect, Type and Tag dropdowns and the favourite / wishlist buttons of the inventory, and the Type, Brand, Series and Material dropdowns of the catalogue: they are now pills in the search bar. The printer views keep their own filters.
