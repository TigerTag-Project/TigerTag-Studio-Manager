Delete your account from Studio, pick your Countries League flag from a window, a redone Invite view, and Happy Hare / AFC editing on Klipper printers.

## Added

- **Delete my account.** A "Delete my account" row at the bottom of the Sign-in section of the account window opens a window listing what will be deleted. You confirm it is you (password, or Google once more), type the confirm word of your language, then hold the red button.
- **Countries League flag picker.** A "Change" button next to your current flag opens a window with a search and every country as a round flag. Nothing changes until you hold the button.
- **30-day flag lock.** In the Countries League, the flag can be changed once every 30 days. The first flag and the first change are free, and the account window shows the date the change opens again.
- **See another country's league.** Click a country in the countries ranking of the TigerSquad League to show its top in the middle column.
- **Klipper: edit Happy Hare gates and AFC lanes.** In the Filament card of a Klipper printer, click a gate or a lane to set its material and colour. Studio writes it to the machine and reads it back.
- **Invite view: more networks.** WeChat, Messenger, LINE, KakaoTalk, VK and SMS join the share buttons.
- **Treasure hunt.** Every sticker shows the share of users who own it. The Locked tab splits into "With a hint" and "Hidden", and a sticker with a hint opens its card.
- **A new secret to find** in the app.

## Changed

- The Invite view is redone: every network is shown at once, the link and its Copy button share one bar, the steps are drawn as a line under the banner, and "Your invitations" is a table (friend, joined, account created, e-mail confirmed, envelopes received).
- The "My code" card is identical in the Friends panel and the Invite view. "New code" now asks for confirmation first.
- Invite links now open the sign-up page of tigersystem.io, which creates your friend's account and records who invited them.
- The app behind the Friends panel is no longer darkened.
- Container picker and Spool details: hovering a container photo shows it enlarged.
- Duplicate on a spool without a container now opens the container picker instead of making copies with a wrong tare weight.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where "View all" in the TigerSquad League shrank the two other columns to 5 rows.
- Fixed an issue where the Friends panel was narrower than intended, and where its code card buttons were cut off or spilled out on a short window.
- Fixed an issue where the Treasure hunt filters in a friend's view showed your own stickers.
- Fixed an issue where giving an envelope from the opening stage showed an older window without the hand of envelopes.
- Fixed an issue where demo printers did not keep or show back the filament written to them (Bambu Lab, Creality, Elegoo, Snapmaker, Anycubic, FlashForge).
