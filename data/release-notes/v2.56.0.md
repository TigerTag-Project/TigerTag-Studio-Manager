Scan a TigerQR label with your computer's camera, add a filament from a QR code, new spools come with the empty spool their product is sold on, and the AI assistant's support requests open in Tiger Studio's own request card.

## Added

- **Scan a QR code with the camera.** A new QR button at the end of the search bar opens a side card with the live camera and a scanner overlay. When a TigerTag QR code is read, a beep plays and the spool (or the product) opens. A QR code that is not a TigerTag link turns the scanner red for a second, then scanning resumes. If the camera sends no picture, the card says so.
- **Add from QR code.** The add chooser has a new "Add from QR code" tile. Scan a filament's QR code and its catalogue card opens, ready to add to your inventory (and to link to any waiting chips).
- **A new spool comes with its empty spool.** When a spool is created from a catalogue product, a QR link or an AI assistant, Tiger Studio now picks the empty spool that product is sold on and applies your weight correction. A container you chose yourself is never replaced.
- **The empty spool list is kept up to date from the server.** A new empty spool added to the TigerTag database shows up in Tiger Studio without waiting for a new version.

## Changed

- **AI assistant requests:** when an assistant prepares a support request or a reply, Tiger Studio opens its own "Improvements & suggestions" card, filled in. You can add and annotate screenshots, attach files or a printer's data, and send it, or not.
- **AI assistant page:** "On the web or my phone" now comes first everywhere (welcome page, setup, dashboard, Access and security), and a new setup starts on Claude online. The two ways to plug in are renamed "Anywhere, online" and "With Tiger Studio open", with "Recommended" and "More powerful" pills.
- **Compare the two:** a new window sets "Anywhere, online" and "With Tiger Studio open" side by side, row by row, with what each can do today.
- **Labels:** the nine label formats are now laid out three per row.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where an error ("Object has been destroyed") could appear when quitting Tiger Studio with a USB scale plugged in. Thanks to Ptitlouis6012 for the fix.
- Fixed an issue where a damaged QR link could cause an error instead of being read as a label from a newer version.
- Fixed an issue where the ticks in the AI assistant's comparison window were white instead of green.
- Fixed an issue where the AI assistant's support messages named the old "Bug or idea?" button instead of "Improvements & suggestions".
- Fixed an issue where a harmless but confusing "Unknown dataset key" message was logged at launch.
