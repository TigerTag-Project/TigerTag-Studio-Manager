The printer views get the search-bar filters, and fixes for the to-order cart, racks, the league flag and the Bambu Lab camera (experimental).

## Added

- **Filters in the printer views.** Brand, Model, State (online / offline) and Tag are now pills written in the search bar, like the spool filters, each "is" or "is not" where it makes sense. They have their own list, so a spool filter never filters your printers.

## Changed

- The list of fields in the search bar opens under the text cursor instead of at the left edge of the bar.
- The brand table bundled with the app is refreshed.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where a product with spools in stock read "0 in stock" in the To-order cart, the red badge, the favourites table and the low-stock notices.
- Fixed an issue where spools freed by shrinking a rack disappeared from both the rack grid and "Spools not stored".
- Fixed an issue where spools stuck at a slot their rack no longer has stayed out of sight. They now return to "Spools not stored".
- Fixed an issue where the league flag in your profile showed a country the league was not using. It now shows the league you are ranked in, and tells you when your new pick takes over.
- Experimental: Bambu Lab cameras that are slow to send their first picture (for example the P2S) are no longer restarted while they are still starting. Tested on a simulator only, not yet on a real printer or Bambu Cloud: please report if your camera stays black.

## Removed

- The Brand, State and Tag dropdowns of the printer views: they are now pills in the search bar.
