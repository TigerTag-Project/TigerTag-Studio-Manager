Steadier printer cameras, search and filters in your requests, and screenshots that open where you can see them.

## New

- **Requests: search and filters.** In "My requests" and "All requests", search by title, number or product, and filter by status, product and type. Each request shows which product it was sent to.
- **Team notices.** TigerMasters are notified when the author of an open request writes in it.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where every printer camera flickered while Studio was starting.
- Fixed an issue where connecting or disconnecting a printer from its side panel made the other printers' cameras flicker.
- Fixed an issue where a screenshot taken from a Tiger Scale or Tiger Touch live screen opened behind the live view.
- Fixed an issue where the search bar's filter list showed on top of a side card.
- Reference data refreshed: 24,947 products in the catalogue.
