An AI assistant page to plug Claude, ChatGPT and others into your stock, Excel export, the Bambu Lab H3C, and a batch of community contributions.

## Added

- **AI assistant (TigerSquad perk).** A new AI assistant page in the sidebar connects Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, Le Chat, Grok or another assistant to your inventory. On this computer, the assistant sees your printers live (the current job, its progress, the filament loaded in each slot) and, when you allow it, can change your stock: weigh, tag and move spools, edit prices and buy links, create spools and racks, print or save a label. Every change is listed with its before and after and can be undone, and nothing is ever deleted. Online, the assistant works anywhere, even with Tiger Studio closed, and can only read. Access is read-only by default and can be cut at any time; leaving the TigerSquad disconnects every assistant.
- **Guide mode.** The assistant can show you a view, open a spool or a printer and make a spool glow, and take a screenshot in which IP addresses, e-mails and keys are blurred first. It never presses a button for you. Off by default, with its own switch under Access and security, and a banner with a Stop button while it acts.
- **Help from the wiki.** The assistant answers from the TigerSystem wiki and can look through your existing support requests. When a problem is not covered, it prepares a request and shows you the exact text, with personal data removed. Nothing is sent until you press Send.
- **Catalogue, invoices and prints.** The assistant can find catalogue products by EAN or SKU, material and colour, identify a spool from the text of its QR code, and check a print's filaments against your stock, with the spool to use, whether there is enough, and what to buy for the rest. Products come with their picture.
- **Excel export.** Settings → Data → "Export .xlsx" writes the whole inventory as a spreadsheet: one line per physical spool with type, material, brand, name, colours, weight, capacity, price, buy link, tags, rack, position, last update and UID. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012 (#193).
- **Close to the notification area (Windows and Linux).** A new setting in Settings → Window, off by default: the close button hides the window and a notification-area icon brings it back. Printers and cameras keep running. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012 (#234).
- **IP camera on a printer.** In a printer's settings, "Camera shown" lets you replace the printer's camera with one of your IP cameras, on its card, in its panel and on the camera wall. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012 (#223).
- **Bambu Lab H3C.** The nine-tool changer is supported, with one temperature pill per tool (T1 to T9) and a target for each, the heated chamber, and the AMS 3H with its nine bays and its two drying zones. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012 (#236).
- **Leaving the TigerSquad** now asks you to type a word before the hold button unlocks, and lists the AI assistant among what is disconnected.
- **"On the scale"** on a spool without a container now asks you to pick its container first.

## Changed

- The Tiger Quests button is no longer in the sidebar; Quests open from the daily and level chips of the view bar.
- The AI cards of My profile are replaced by a single card that leads to the AI assistant page.
- The Printers board's camera widget is removed: it repeated the live feed each machine card already shows. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012 (#223).

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where the Temperature widget on the Printers board grew wider than its machine card and stuck out of its column. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012 (#222).
- Fixed an issue where the light of an Anycubic Kobra S1 could not be switched on. Thanks to @Ptitlouis6012 (#196).
- Fixed an issue where printed labels (PDF, PNG, SVG and Print) failed in Tiger Studio Web.
- Fixed an issue where a spool with a generic NTAG chip was reset every time the chip was scanned (#227).
- Fixed an issue where a long press on a hold-to-confirm button opened the browser's menu on a phone and cancelled the hold in Tiger Studio Web (#231).
- Fixed an issue where Tiger Studio Web did not follow a new release automatically (#209).
- Fixed an issue where the .ttag import preview showed a TigerData+ material as TigerData.
- Fixed an issue where the colour range bars showed a resize cursor while a press moved the whole selection. The cursor now matches what the press does, and a sideways scroll slides the selection.
- Fixed an issue where a Bons plans deal's end date showed one day late, and where "Code claimed" was shown in orange instead of green.
