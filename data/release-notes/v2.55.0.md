Online AI assistants can now change your stock, any filament or spool can be shared with a short link, QR labels get their TigerQR pill, and iPhone photos work as an avatar.

## Added

- **Online assistants can change your stock.** ChatGPT, Claude on the web and the other online assistants can now read and write, not only read. You pick "Read" or "Read & write" when you connect, and you can switch it per assistant in Manage access. Every change an online assistant makes shows up in Tiger Studio's history under its name, next to the ones made on this computer, and can be undone with one click. The daily limits now count from midnight UTC.
- **Gemini in the AI assistant page.** Two new tiles: Gemini CLI (on this computer; "Copy the command" gives the line to paste in a terminal) and Gemini (on the web or your phone, with Google's current limits spelled out).
- **An "Open" button on the last setup step.** While Tiger Studio waits for the connection, a button opens Claude, Cursor, VS Code, the terminal (for Gemini CLI) or the assistant's connectors page.
- **A sliding Read | Read & write switch** replaces the two access cards in the AI setup and in Access and security.
- **"Plug in another assistant"** now brings back the welcome page with the assistant tiles and the demo, with a Back button.
- **Share a filament or a spool.** A new Share button on the catalogue product card, on your own materials, on the spool detail and in the label card opens a window with a short link in your language, a "Copy link" button and the same network buttons as the Invite view. A filament shares its product page, a spool shares its own page.
- **A pasted or scanned QR link opens what it points to.** Someone else's spool opens its product with the line "Someone else's spool. Here's what's on it."; a private spool says so; a product link opens its catalogue card. A handheld scanner is read when it presses Enter, never halfway through a link.
- **New label template "QR only"** (25 × 30 mm): the QR code with its TigerQR pill above it.
- **"Copy link" under the label preview** copies the link the label's QR opens.
- **Instagram, Discord and Messenger shares** copy the message and open the app's web inbox, so you only have to paste.
- **The Invite view** gets an Instagram button, and the network logos are drawn without a card around them.

## Changed

- **Labels:** the pill in the corner now reads TigerQR+ when the spool is a catalogue product (and always on a Filament Label), TigerQR otherwise. It used to show the chip protocol.
- **Product QR links** now print the product number on 10 digits. Labels printed before still scan.
- **Referrals:** you and the friend you invited each get your envelopes once the friend has opened an app and added a first spool, not when the e-mail is confirmed. A referrer can be paid up to 10 times a month. The Invite view shows a fourth step and tells you under each invitation why it is not paid yet, and the invited friend sees "Add your first spool: your envelopes are waiting".
- **A referral no longer makes the two accounts friends.** They send a friend request like anyone else.
- A friend or member who never chose a pseudo now shows as "New member" instead of a long identifier.
- **The AI assistant** is named "TigerSystem.io" in your assistants, and the Claude Desktop extension card now says MIT and read-only unless you choose Read & write.
- When an assistant is refused outside the TigerSquad, it now tells you which TigerSystem account the connection is signed in to, and where to join.
- The QR icon is the one used in Tiger NFC Connect.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where choosing an unreadable picture as avatar did nothing; Tiger Studio now says why (unreadable, too large, upload failed).
- Fixed an issue where iPhone photos (HEIC) could not be used as an avatar. On macOS they now convert on the fly; on Windows, Linux and Tiger Studio Web a message explains they cannot be opened there.
- Fixed an issue where an upload error was reported as "Your profile couldn't be saved".
- Fixed an issue where the AI assistant's change history only kept 300 changes, below its own 500-a-day limit; it now keeps 600.
- Fixed an issue where an assistant creating spools could write a note too long for the chip, create an empty spool when a full one was asked for, or create the same spools again after a failure midway.
- Fixed an issue where an assistant's product change refused by the server was reported as done.
