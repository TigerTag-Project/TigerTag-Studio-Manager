Printed QR labels for your spools and filaments, scanned labels that open the right spool, and a way to restore a worn TigerTag+.

## Added

- **Printed QR labels.** Print a label for a spool (Spool Label) or for a filament of the catalogue (Filament Label), in eight formats: Classic 40×30, Wide 50×30, Compact 40×25, Slim 40×12, Slicer-friendly 40×30, Vertical 30×40, Vertical bold 30×40 and Vertical compact 25×40 mm. Each label carries the brand, the series, the colour name and its hex codes, up to four values of your choice (nozzle, bed, drying, TD, diameter) and a QR code. Print it directly, or save it as PDF, PNG (203 or 300 dpi) or SVG.
- **QR buttons.** A QR button next to the star opens the label from the spool card, the grouped spools card and the toolbox; the product sheet has a new "Swatch Card Label" tab, and the catalogue product card prints the Filament Label.
- **Scanned labels open the spool.** The QR code of a Spool Label opens that spool in Tiger Studio for its owner, from Studio Web, from the desktop app or when the link is pasted into the search. A link carrying a new weight or note proposes it on the spool card; a long press saves it.
- **Restore TigerTag+.** A worn or wiped TigerTag+ can be rewritten from its backup: place it on the TigerPOD, choose "Restore TigerTag+" in the new-chip window, check what will be written back, then hold the button.

## Changed

- New spools without a chip get a longer, collision-free id, and a spool that later gets a chip keeps opening from a label printed before.
- The choices of the new-chip window stay visible in a small window.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where a freshly read TigerTag+ could lose its backup badge although its backup exists.
