A redrawn treasure hunt, a TigerSquad that asks you to update, 30 days to think before you leave it, and a much faster app when you move between views.

## Added

- **Treasure hunt.** The sticker album has a new name and a new look: an explorer's temple banner with a counter of the stickers you have collected. Some stickers stay veiled until a friend holds them, and you can browse a friend's album from their TigerSquad view.
- **The TigerSquad has evolved.** When the TigerSquad needs a newer version of the app, its views and actions pause and a window offers to update or restart.
- **Leaving the TigerSquad now has a 30-day wait.** Before you switch it off, a window lists what goes and what stays. If you come back within the 30 days, the TigerSquad tells you the date your access returns.
- **How streaks and freezes work.** The streak card and the freeze card each have an info button that opens a clear window with the rules, a milestone table and a picture of every item mentioned.
- **Give a freeze** by clicking one of the freeze cubes on the card.
- **Give envelopes your way.** Pick which envelopes to give from a hand of up to 10, and give several at once. A friend who receives several gets a single notification and opens the whole pile.
- **Requests.** A Copy button on every message of a conversation, an open-requests count on the All requests tab, and a Requests tab in the notification centre for the team, with a card for each new request.
- **Launch splash.** A new start-up screen with the ecosystem scene, a loading line in your language and a progress bar that follows the real start-up steps.
- **Invite a friend, clearer.** The reward is in the title, there is one main button to copy your link, your code comes second, and the QR code can be enlarged.

## Changed

- Send TigerCoins: at least 20 coins per envelope, and one envelope a day to the same friend.
- The app starts faster and reads far less data: the inventory and the sticker albums are kept on your computer and only what changed is fetched.
- Moving between views is faster. Visiting a friend, coming back home, the TigerSquad rankings and the requests list draw at once from what you saw last.
- The view bar shows the last session's figures right at start-up instead of filling in late.
- In a friend's view, the header shows only their counts: their stock weight and value are no longer displayed.
- The Missions view is named "Missions" instead of "Tutorial missions".
- Light theme: greys, daily-quest icons, badges, banners and rankings are easier to read.
- Every illustration of the quests, TigerCoins and TigerSquad is now in high definition.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where the Missions cards were squeezed six to a row on a narrower window, with titles broken word by word.
- Fixed an issue where the Secret mission card was unreadable in the Light theme.
- Fixed an issue where All requests listed requests already closed on GitHub as open (#181).
- Fixed an issue where the light band across a sticker did not show.
