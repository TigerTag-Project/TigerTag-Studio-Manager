Tiger Quests rank 3 "Mountaineer", the Refill badge in the catalogue, the catalogue's series and colour names back, and fixes to .ttag import and trophy windows.

## Added

- **Tiger Quests: Mountaineer rank.** A third rank with five levels of social missions: envelopes, freezes and TigerCoins between friends, hidden envelopes, the sticker album, the Makers League, and the purchase price and buy links of your products. What you already did before this version counts.
- **Refill badge in the catalogue.** Refill products show their Refill badge in the catalogue grid and list, before opening the product.

## Changed

- Refill, twin and backup badges now sit at the top left of every picture (cards, tables, spool panel, product card), so each badge is in the same corner everywhere.
- Spools tied to a catalogue product now refresh when any field of that product changes, not only its photo.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where the catalogue had lost its series, colour names, aspects and change dates since 1 September 2026. They are back, and installed catalogues refresh once.
- Fixed an issue where a spool thumbnail showed a grey square instead of the spool colour while its photo loaded.
- Fixed an issue where importing a .ttag file turned a TigerData+ or a TigerTag+ into a plain TigerData instead of keeping its product.
- Fixed an issue where the "Trophy unlocked" window did not appear for some hidden trophies.
- Fixed an issue where trophy stickers looked like several stickers stacked, instead of one piece with a single white edge.
