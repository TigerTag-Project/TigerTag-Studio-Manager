Choose your profile colours (Solid, Bicolor, Rainbow or Aurora), pick them with a colour picker that opens where you click, see RAL and Pantone codes on products and spools, and add spools from a TigerTag or a TigerQR+ straight from the catalogue.

## Added

- **Colour styles for your profile.** In your profile window, choose Solid, Bicolor, Rainbow or Aurora for your avatar and your theme, with up to three colours. Bicolor is available to TigerSquad members, Rainbow from Gold and Aurora from Platinum. Smoothness and Intensity sliders set how the colours blend and how strong they are. Other members' league rows show their colours too.
- **A colour picker that opens next to where you click**, instead of the system colour panel.
- **RAL and Pantone codes.** When the brand provides them, the catalogue product card and the spool card show the RAL and Pantone code of each colour (RAL with its name and an approximate swatch).
- **Add with TigerTag.** A new view in the add chooser reads the TigerTag on your TigerPOD and adds the spool linked to it. A scanned chip that is not yet in your inventory now opens its product card instead of being created automatically, with an "Add with TigerTag" button.
- **Add from the catalogue and Add with TigerQR+.** On a catalogue product card, two new held buttons add the spool without a chip, or add it and open its QR label.
- **TigerQR+ trophies** for scanning a TigerQR+ with the camera and adding a material from one.
- **World league top 100.** The worldwide TigerSquad ranking now shows its first 100 members.

## Changed

- The profile window is shorter: the profile photo card now shows the live preview, with the TigerSquad title, XP and your display name.
- The My profile card columns are balanced.
- The catalogue shows one figure ("20886 products") placed before the Select button.
- Grid cards have a line between the colour picture and the text.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where a deleted spool stayed on screen until the app was reloaded.
- Fixed an issue where adding a spool from its chip was slower than needed because it was saved in several steps.
