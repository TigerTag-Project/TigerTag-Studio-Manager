Brand logos on your printed labels, chips that update themselves on the TigerPOD, the Creality K3 and SparkX i8, and a catalogue Material filter that groups every variant under its base material.

## Added

- **Brand logos on labels.** When the brand has a logo in the TigerTag catalogue, your printed labels show it in black instead of the brand name. Over a hundred brands have one; the others keep their name.
- **Chips update themselves on the TigerPOD.** When a spool's chip needs an update (its product changed in the catalogue, or you edited something stored on the chip), placing the spool on the TigerPOD now rewrites the chip right away and checks it. No button to press. A TigerTag+ Certified chip keeps its certification.
- **Creality K3.** Detection on your network, its picture, the four nozzle temperatures (the active nozzle can be set) and its CFS Nano units. It has no external spool holder, so none is shown.
- **Creality SparkX i8.** Detection on your network, its picture and its four-slot rack. It has no external spool holder, so none is shown.
- **Polymaker in the Snapmaker filament list**, with its own materials (PLA, PETG, ABS, TPU, ASA, PC, PA, PCTG, PE, PHA, PVA, BVOH, HIPS, EVA).

## Changed

- The catalogue's Material filter now lists base materials only (PLA, PETG, ABS…). "PLA" includes every PLA: silk, marble, matte and the others. Type "silk" in the search to narrow it down.
- When your search finds products but the catalogue filters hide them all, the catalogue now says so and offers to clear the filters, instead of suggesting to create the product.
- The catalogue bundled with the app is up to date (21,512 products).

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where a Bambu Lab printer connected through the cloud showed no picture of the print in progress, after Bambu Lab blocked the identity the app used for its cloud requests.

## Removed

- The "Copy API URL" button in Settings → Data. To read your data from another tool, sign in to your TigerTag account.
