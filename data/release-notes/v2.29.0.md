Add a whole fleet from one scan, and Bambu Lab accounts from mainland China.

## Added

- **Add several printers at once** — the network scan now lets you tick one or more of the printers it finds, for every brand, and add them in one go. When a printer needs its own access or check code, you type it right on its card. Ticking a single printer opens the settings form as before.
- **Bambu Lab accounts on the China mainland platform** — pick "China mainland" when signing in, and use your phone number: the code arrives by SMS.

## Changed

- Signing in to Bambu Lab now explains what is wrong when the email or phone number can't be used, instead of doing nothing.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where, while adding a printer, the next step's panel slid in behind the previous one before appearing.
