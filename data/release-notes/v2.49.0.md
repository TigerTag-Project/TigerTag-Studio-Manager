Trophies to collect, friends to invite, TigerCoins to send, and your requests become real conversations.

## Added

- **Trophies.** A sticker album in the Tiger Squad group: 116 trophies to start with — streaks and freezes, TigerCoins, friends, chips, workshop gear, every Workshop item, Tiger devices, tiers for coins, envelopes and freezes sent and received, one set per brand selling TigerTag+ Certified spools (Rosa3D, eSun, Sunlu, Jamg He, R3D, Landu, Lotactree, Smart3DPT), hidden ones, and event trophies unlocked with a code. Each earned sticker shows its date and how many users own it, and can be downloaded. A new trophy opens a celebration window and a notification.
- **Invite a friend.** A new Invite view in the Tiger Squad group with your friend link, share buttons (WhatsApp, X, Facebook, Reddit, Telegram, Bluesky, Discord, e-mail) and a QR code. A new account can say who invited it during the first 7 days; once the new account's e-mail address is confirmed, both get 3 gift envelopes. Also reachable from the Friends panel, the sidebar friends list and the account menu. Six trophies come with it.
- **Send TigerCoins to a friend** from the Friends panel: pick a TigerSquad friend, an amount within your weekly allowance (by TigerSquad level) and a message, check the summary, then hold to send. The friend receives an envelope with the coins.
- **Envelopes with a message open as a letter**: the seal breaks, the message unrolls with your friend's signature, and holding "Collect" sends the coins to your TigerCoins counter.
- **Report a message** received with TigerCoins, with the option to block and remove the sender in the same step.
- **Give streak freezes from the Friends panel**: each friend shows their freeze slots, and a free slot opens the freeze window on that friend.
- **Request conversations.** In My requests, each request opens its conversation: read the team's replies and answer from the app, with text, pictures, PDFs and a printer's data, no GitHub account needed. New replies are flagged, and a window tells you when a request moved.
- **All requests** is open to every member: read everyone's requests, and answer the questions as the community.
- **Chest opening**: chests now have their own stage, music and a held "Open the chest", with the envelopes rising out one by one.
- New achievement **Chests opened**: +200 XP per chest.
- **Sign-in card in My profile** (#176): see your e-mail and how you sign in; e-mail accounts can change their address or password.
- Lotactree masterspool containers (White 150 g, Transparent 194 g) in the container picker (#165).
- Demo printer Elegoo Centauri Carbon running OpenCentauri COSMOS, with its four-lane CANVAS.

## Changed

- Tiger Quests: a broken streak is now relit with "Relight the flame", with a ceremony and the days coming back; the strip sits at the bottom of the streak card.
- Tiger Quests: drawn streak flames, freezes to give that melt day by day, "Delivery in 4 days" for the next freezes, and the freeze rules in an info bubble with one row per TigerSquad level.
- Tiger Quests: the streak card shows the current week as seven days; finishing the day's set opens a "Daily set complete" window wherever you are.
- Freeze windows (buy, give, relight) and the Send TigerCoins window redesigned: friends in one row, one held button, and a clear hint on what is missing.
- A friend's envelope opens right in its own window, with their avatar on the seal.
- The TigerCoins history puts your balance first, with a Send button; opened chests have their own picture.
- The header and TigerCoins windows show your last figures at once at launch, then update.
- The four legendary devices in the Workshop open their presentation sheet.
- The requests window is wider, and "Later" on the request alert brings it back 30 minutes later.

## Fixed

- Fixed an issue where avatars without a photo showed their initials off-centre in the send and freeze windows and on envelope seals.
- Fixed an issue where a new, empty account showed an empty header.
- Fixed an issue where the TigerCoins card did not appear after joining the TigerSquad until the next launch.
- Fixed an issue where the week's days stayed dim for a streak that started before the history was recorded.
- Fixed an issue where singular and plural forms were wrong in some languages, such as Polish and Russian.
- Fixed an issue where a spool already linked to its own Bambu Lab / Creality chips was offered to link other chips placed on the TigerPOD.
- Fixed an issue where the "Turn on debug mode" quest only counted at the next launch.
- Fixed an issue where Klipper printers with an AFC unit listing each lane twice showed every filament twice (#167).
