Welcome to your workshop 2.0.

The workshop of the future: RFID chips, weighing, printers live and shared stock, all in one place.

  • The right spool, no digging. Scan any RFID chip — TigerTag, Bambu Lab, Creality… — and its whole card shows up.
  • News from your printers. Status and progress of every print, live.
  • Stock gets shared. So does PLA. Share your inventory and wishlists with friends.
  • Maker deals. Real savings. Filament, parts and gear at TigerSquad prices, plus vouchers earned with your TigerCoins.

No TigerPOD needed to get started.
